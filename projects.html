Give it a prompt, get back a whole batch of podcast episodes. Scripts come from the Anthropic API; the voices come from a pluggable TTS adapter layer, so swapping Fish Audio for ElevenLabs is a config change rather than a rewrite. The interesting part is the concurrency — a 100-episode run is bounded by an asyncio semaphore so it saturates without falling over. SQLite behind it, deployed on Vercel.

A DJ library gets messy fast, and the mess is a sorting problem. I’m building two pieces that work together: a gamified Sorting Deck that previews a track on hover, guesses its category from BPM and energy, and lets me confirm or correct it with a keystroke; and a drag-and-drop Set Builder that assembles a tracklist and flags jarring BPM jumps between tracks. It works on whatever is already in my folders — a scan picks up new files and queues them for sorting. Audio analysis runs on librosa for BPM, key and energy, with mutagen reading the ID3 tags.

Electron · React · Vite · Python · FastAPI · librosa · SQLite
